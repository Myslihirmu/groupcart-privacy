GroupCart is a shared, real-time shopping list for families and groups. Add items together, get a heads-up before someone heads to the store, and never buy the same thing twice.

Everything a shared list should be

Built for the way households actually shop — together, and rarely in the same place.

Shared in real time

Everyone in your group sees the same list update instantly. No more double-buying the milk.

🔔

A heads-up before the store

Tap the bell to tell your group you're heading out — now or at a set time — so they add what they need first.

📖

Recipes to list in one tap

Save your group's recipes and add all their ingredients to the shopping list at once.

⚡

Quick Add favourites

Keep the things you always buy a single tap away. New accounts even start with the basics.

📱

Join in seconds

Share an invite link or let people scan a QR code to join a group instantly.

Speaks your language

English, Finnish, Swedish, German, Spanish and French — chosen automatically from your phone.

How it works

Create or join a group

Start a group for your household and invite others with a link or a QR code.

Build the list together

Anyone can add items, notes and recipes. Everything syncs for the whole group.

Shop in sync

Get a heads-up when someone's going, check items off, and keep a tidy shopping history.

Made for groups, everywhere

Families, flatmates, partners, teams — if you share a fridge or a list, GroupCart keeps everyone on the same page.
